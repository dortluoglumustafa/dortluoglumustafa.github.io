About My Background

Hello world, I'm Mustafa Dortluoğlu. I am a math enthusiast since middle school and my major shift became evident when I embarked upon mathematical olympiads. Through participating in ISBO and TÜBİTAK's training camps, I've developed my skills and obtained the chance to compete in international competitions by joining the national olympiad team. I've achieved 2 times silver medal in the IMO. Nevertheless, I aspire for higher mathematics more than olympiad. I participated in MBL (Math Beyond Limits) Balkan and MBL Poland 2025, which were one of the most transformative experiences for me, where my perspective on the range of mathematical disciplines have widened and grew. In so little time, I was able to learn the introductions of so many mathematical courses that it was really fascinating. The topics ranged from complex analysis to abstract algebra to topology. MBL is a growing society that is becoming popular in the olympiad community and if this is your first time check it out: MBL
