What is our aim through the year?

We aim to cover various undergraduate level mathematical courses spanning real analysis, group theory, topology and more. There will also be sessions for interesting individual topics such as cyclatomic polynomials, gaussian integers, and projective geometry. From time to time, we will hold sessions for revisal of previous weeks where we will solve problems from a couple lessons back. Nevertheless, this club is just for enjoying the art of mathematics, and it doesn't bring any academic stress. We are flexible in our goals, but still hope to achieve the most through the year!
