Welcome to the Advance Math Club!

Why and How?

Advance Math Club was created by Mustafa Dortluoğlu in 2026 at Robert College İstanbul. This page is currently an attempt to what might eventually grow into the official club's webpage. I am proud to be teaching mathematical concepts that exceed the highschool curriculum. Personally, school mathematics didn't satisfy me, and believing that others might be feeling the same, I initiated this club to benefit all who aspire to advance their mathematical knowledge, either for fun or for future career.
